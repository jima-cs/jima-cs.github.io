Hi! I am Ji Ma (马骥). I am a CS Ph.D. candidate at the State Key Lab of CAD&CG,
[Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/), supervised by
[Prof. Yingcai Wu](http://www.ycwu.org/) and [Prof. Jiachen Wang](https://wjc-vis.com).
 My research interests lie in AI4Sports and Human-Centric AI.
 I focus on incorporating professional domain knowledge into data-driven models to democratize elite sports expertise.
  Specifically, I utilized multimodal sensing, embodied AI and LLMs to enable intelligent coaching and automated tactical analysis in table tennis.

I served as the chief architect of the CTTT’s Intelligent Big Data Platform and International Tournament Management System, aiding the national team in preparing for major international competitions.

I love running. I led the effort to establish the Kuafu Running Club as a registered student organization at Zhejiang University and served as its inaugural president.
 We maintain a community of more than 2,300 student runners.
